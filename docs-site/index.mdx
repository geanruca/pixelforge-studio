## How it works

1. **Estimate.** The price of an image is only known once it is done, so you get a range: minimum, expected and maximum.
2. **Create.** The maximum price is reserved from your credits and the generation is queued. The call returns at once.
3. **Poll.** Read the generation until its status is `succeeded` or `failed`. It usually takes 5 to 30 seconds, and up to about a minute and a half.
4. **Settle.** On success you are charged the actual price and the rest of the reservation returns to your balance. A failed generation costs nothing.

## Conventions

- Base URL: `https://pixelforge.24.199.64.10.sslip.io/api/v1`
- Requests and responses are JSON.
- Money is an **exact decimal string** in US dollars with nine decimals, such as `"0.070625000"`. It is never a float: parse it with a decimal type.
- Timestamps are ISO 8601 in UTC.
- 60 requests per minute per account. See [Errors](/errors) for `rate_limit_exceeded`.
